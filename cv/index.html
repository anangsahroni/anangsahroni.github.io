Summary Data analyst and software developer with a background in physics and geophysics. From 2020 to 2024 I built real-time data pipelines, REST back-ends and dashboards for sensor data from seismic and magnetic instruments; since 2024 I have been analysing laboratory measurement data at LMU Munich.
